Orange Egypt · Data Science team · July 2026

Face recognition access control

An access control system that decides who is allowed in by recognizing faces from a live camera feed. OpenCV captures frames and finds the face in each one, then DeepFace compares it against the faces of enrolled people to confirm who it is. A match grants access and an unknown face is denied.

I built it during my internship at Orange as a working prototype of face-based authentication, and the full code is on GitHub.
